03 About

I'm Joline, an Economics major and Industrial Design minor at Georgia Tech, graduating in May 2027. I'm most drawn to rooms where I have a lot to learn: digging into unfamiliar problems, asking (a lot of dumb) questions, and putting what I learn to work.

I've previously interned across both early- and late-stage tech startups, and I'm currently looking for new grad opportunities in product management, product strategy, and business operations.

It's safe to say my path here hasn't exactly been conventional. I've moved between universities and was rejected by Georgia Tech three times as a transfer before eventually getting in—the very school I'll graduate from next year. Somewhere along the way, I got pretty comfortable being uncomfortable.

For me, I don't need to know that something will work to believe it's worth trying. I'd rather take the shot, learn from what happens, and try again than be left wondering, “what if?”
